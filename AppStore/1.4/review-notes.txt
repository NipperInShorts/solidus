Solidus is a simple percent-off and percent-of calculator; no account or sign-in is required.

Version 1.4 adds an adaptive iPhone Duo workspace, with calculation inputs and keypad positioned around the active display division. The app also supports portrait and landscape layouts on regular iPhones.

To test: enter 25 for Percent, tap Amount and enter 128. Off displays 96.00; Of displays 32.00. Clear all is inside the darker keypad area.
